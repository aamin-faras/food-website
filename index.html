Our Heritage

Passion Born in Florence,
Perfected for Modern Taste.

Founded in 2012 by Executive Chef Mateo Rossi, L’Aura began with a solitary clay oven and a relentless dedication to Mediterranean craftsmanship. We work with local artisan growers and regional foragers to curate ingredients that honor each season.
